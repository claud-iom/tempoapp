Privacy policy

Who we are

Tempo is a free iPhone app from Tempo Check LLC, a California limited liability company. The public site is tempoapp.us. Questions go to admin@tempoapp.us.

What Tempo is

Tempo shows how busy a listed bar, sports bar, club, restaurant, or brewery seemed, based on what people post. A check is one word: Quiet, Building, Busy, or Packed. A short comment is optional. A check on a place counts for 90 minutes. After that, the place shows No recent check. That means no report in the last 90 minutes. It does not mean the room is empty, safe, or unsafe. Tempo does not visit the place, does not use a camera or an occupancy sensor, and does not verify the word. Tempo does not own, run, or represent the places on the map. A name on the map identifies the place. It is not a partnership. You can look without posting and without turning location on.

On this phone

In this build, Sign in with Apple, Sign in with Google, and the phone-number screen do not create an account with Tempo Check LLC. A phone number, a backup email, and a birthday typed on those screens are not sent to the company. Your name, checks, friends, requests, blocks, and settings stay in the app session on that phone. The company has no copy. Alerts are not sent. Sample checks on place pages are part of the app. They are not live reports from other people. A check you post is not delivered to anyone else’s phone. Delete account clears that session and signs you out. Sign out leaves the session until the app stops.

When accounts leave the phone

A later build may store an account with the company. This policy will be updated before that build ships. Until then, the sections below describe that later service. They do not describe data the company already holds.

Your name

Other people who use Tempo see your first initial and last name on a check. A person who accepts a friend request sees your full first and last name, including on your checks. You always see your own full name. People do not see your phone number or email on a check.

Birthday

Tempo asks for a date of birth once, to decide whether you are 21 or older. A date under 21 stops the app. The date is one you type. Tempo does not check a government ID. The intended design keeps the fact that the account is 21 or older, and does not keep the date itself.

Sign-in

You will be able to use Apple, Google, or a phone number. Phone sign-in also asks for a backup email. Apple or Google receives the sign-in you start with them. The company that sends a login text receives the phone number, and only for that code. Those companies will be named in this policy on the day they first receive data. Tempo does not keep a profile photo from a sign-in service.

A check

You supply the word and any comment. One current check per person per place. A new word replaces your previous check at that place. For 90 minutes, other people using Tempo can see the word, any comment, the place, and the time, with your first initial and last name, whether or not they are at the place. After 90 minutes the place shows No recent check. Your profile can still list that check until you replace it or delete the account. Who can see your check count, place count, and friend count follows Public, Friends only, or Hidden. You always see your own. That setting does not take your name off a check during the 90 minutes it is on the place. A check is a record that this account reported this place at this time. It is not a GPS trail, and it is not proof you went inside.

Precise location

After you allow location while the app is open, the phone’s precise location is used to decide whether you are within about 251 meters of a place, to show distance, and to show your position on the map. Posting also requires the app to be open. If location is off, denied, or too uncertain, you cannot post. Tempo does not ask for Always location and does not collect location in the background. Tempo does not keep a coordinate history. The saved check stores the account, the place, and the time. It does not store a latitude and longitude. Precise location used this way is sensitive personal information under California law. The only use is the feature you opened.

Friends and blocks

A friend request stays pending until the other person accepts or denies it. Accept shows full names both ways. Deny puts the button back to Add friend and does not leave a denied label. Other people do not receive your friend list. Block hides that person on places you open and ends a friendship or a pending request. Tempo does not tell them. Block does not remove your name from their app, and it does not stop them from seeing your checks. Block is not a promise of physical safety.

Who receives information

In this build, no one outside the phone. The company has no copy. When accounts leave the phone, recipients are other people using Tempo, Apple or Google if you use them to sign in, the company that sends a login text if you use phone sign-in, the company that hosts the accounts once there is one, and a recipient named in a valid legal demand. A bar, club, restaurant, or brewery does not receive names, phone numbers, emails, or a check history.

Sale and ads

Tempo Check LLC does not sell personal information. Tempo does not share personal information for cross-context advertising. There is no ad tracking and no tracking across other companies’ apps or websites. A check that other people in Tempo can see is a disclosure you make by posting. It is not a sale. If Tempo later offers ads or a sale of information, this policy will change first, and a sale will be a separate choice that stays off until you turn it on. You will still be able to use Tempo if you say no. tempoapp.us does not ask you to create an account, and the company has not installed an analytics or advertising tool on it.

How long it lasts

In this build, the session lasts while the app is running. Delete account clears it on that phone. When accounts leave the phone, coordinates used for the radius check stay in phone memory while the app is open. The word and comment count on the place page for 90 minutes. Your profile can keep the place, the word, and the time until you delete the account. Name, email, phone, sign-in identifier, friends, requests, blocks, and settings last until you delete the account. A denied request is removed. Host backups, once they exist, will be kept no longer than 30 days after deletion. Mail to admin@tempoapp.us is deleted within 24 months after the question is finished. A check already open on someone else’s phone can stay on that screen until they leave it or the 90 minutes end. Tempo cannot pull a screenshot off their phone.

Security

In this build the company cannot read the session. Anyone who can unlock your phone can open Tempo on it. When a host exists, account data will move over an encrypted connection. A posted check is meant to be read by other people in Tempo.

Age

Tempo is for people 21 or older. It is not directed at children. Tempo does not knowingly keep an account for anyone under 21. If we learn an account is under 21, we close it and delete the account data we hold.

Your choices

In the app you can change your name, who can see your stats, notification switches, appearance, friends, and blocks. Sign out leaves the account in place. Delete account removes the name, checks, comments, friends, requests, and blocks held for that account, then signs you out. You can email admin@tempoapp.us to ask for access, a correction, or deletion. We will not ask for a government ID, a Social Security number, or a home address to do that. We will answer within 45 days. Asking does not change the price of Tempo. Tempo is free.

Legal demands

We review subpoenas, court orders, and other lawful demands, and we ask that the demand be limited to what the order requires. We tell you unless the law, the order, or a risk of harm forbids notice. In this build the company has no account server to search.

California

Tempo Check LLC will follow the California Consumer Privacy Act if and when the company meets a statutory threshold. This policy does not say the company already meets one. Showing a check to other people in Tempo is a disclosure. It is not a sale, and it is not sharing for cross-context advertising. A California resident may email admin@tempoapp.us to ask what we hold, to correct it, or to delete it. There is no sale to opt out of.

Outside the United States

Tempo is operated from the United States. If you use it from somewhere else, the information in this policy is handled in the United States.

Changes

We will change the date, post the new text in the app and on tempoapp.us, and show a notice in the app before a change to location, to checks, or to who receives information.
